Getting Started

STEP 1
Sign In
Go to sellersignal.co and click Sign In in the top right. Use the email and password you set up after your onboarding call. You'll land on your Dashboard.

Open Your Briefing

Work Your List
Start with your Act Today prospects. Click "Deep Signal" on any card to see the full psychological profile, approach scripts, and what NOT to say. Use CRM buttons to track your outreach.

Understanding Your Briefing

Every morning, SellerSignal scans every property in your ZIP code against 47 seller indicators. Owners are scored and sorted into three tiers:

Act Today Highest probability. These owners match your market's actual monthly seller turnover rate.
Outreach 3-6 month horizon. Worth a letter or early relationship, not a cold call yet.
Watch Some elevated signals but not enough convergence. Can move up as new data arrives.

Each prospect card shows the owner name, property address, a Street View photo, key signals (owner type, absentee status, equity indicators), and a composite score.

WHAT MAKES SELLERSIGNAL DIFFERENT

Every prospecting tool gives you the same public records. SellerSignal adds the layer nobody else has: Deep Signal — an AI analysis of the person behind the property. Their likely motivation, psychology, timeline, and exactly how to approach them. This is the difference between knocking on a door and knowing what to say when it opens.

Deep Signal

Click the "Deep Signal" button on any prospect card to generate a full intelligence profile. This runs a real-time AI analysis that takes about 15-30 seconds and includes:

Seller Psychology — why this person might sell, what's driving their decision, and their likely emotional state around the transaction.

Recommended Approach — whether to lead with empathy, data, urgency, or relationship-building based on their profile.

Personalized Scripts — ready-to-use language for phone, mail, and door, tailored specifically to this seller's situation.

What NOT to Say — the red warning section. Specific phrases and approaches that would backfire with this particular owner.

CRM & Pipeline

Every prospect card has pipeline buttons: Not Interested, Contacted, Nurturing, Meeting Set, Listed. Click to track your progress. Your pipeline persists across sessions and syncs to the server.

Mail Campaigns

Your subscription includes 10 mail credits. Each credit sends one AI-written letter through USPS — personalized to the specific seller's situation. You can purchase additional credits in packs of 25, 50, or 100 from your account.

Each letter in a campaign is unique. Letter 1 introduces you. Letter 2 builds on the first. By Letter 6, it's a direct, relationship-based ask. Every letter references the seller's specific property and circumstances.

Frequently Asked Questions

GETTING STARTED
How do I claim a territory?

During beta, territories are assigned by invitation. Contact Jeremy directly to be set up in the ZIP code you want. Once claimed, your territory is exclusive — no other SellerSignal agent can operate in it while you hold it.

How often does my data update?

Parcel data, ownership, and scoring refresh nightly. Deep Signal reports for top Act Today prospects are regenerated on a weekly or better cadence. You can also generate a fresh Deep Signal on any prospect on demand — it takes 30-60 seconds.

Can my team or assistant use my account?

During beta, accounts are tied to individual licensed agents. Team access isn't formally supported yet. What if I want to work multiple ZIP codes?

You can claim multiple territories — each is priced separately based on its tier. Your dashboard will show all your active territories, and clicking any of them opens its briefing. There's no bundle discount during beta, but there may be one post-launch.

UNDERSTANDING THE SCORES
How are prospects scored?

Every parcel in your ZIP gets a 0-100 Briefing Rank built from four sub-scores: Seller Likelihood (probability they list in 12 months), Off-Market Receptivity (fit for direct outreach), Actionability (how reachable they are), and Confidence (how much the model trusts the signal).

The scoring engine weighs indicators like absentee ownership, trust/LLC ownership patterns, length of tenure, estate transitions, out-of-state mailing addresses, and market-specific life-event signals. For top prospects, we also run an AI psychology layer (Deep Signal) that generates personalized motivation analysis.

What's the difference between Act Today, Outreach, and Watch?

Act Today is the top prospect count matched to your ZIP's actual monthly turnover rate — if 144 homes sell per month in your area, you see 144 ranked prospects. These are the people most likely to list this month. Open Deep Signal and contact them.

Outreach is the next tier, sized at roughly 2x your Act Today count. These are cultivation targets — mail them the Six Letters program, touch them quarterly, stay top of mind.

Watch is everything else that scored above the noise floor. These move up or down week to week as signals change.

Why are some prospects ranked "Top 0.5%" when their absolute score looks low?

Scores compress differently across markets. A score of 36 in a tightly-distributed market like North Carolina can represent the top 1% of prospects, while in Paradise Valley it's closer to the 30th percentile. The "Top X%" chip on each card tells you how exceptional the prospect is within their specific ZIP, which is more meaningful than the raw number.

Why is the Historical Accuracy panel sometimes missing?

Historical Accuracy is computed from 24 months of actual sales data. If your ZIP doesn't have enough validated transactions yet (minimum 10 sales), the panel is suppressed rather than shown with unreliable numbers. As more data accumulates, the panel will return.

DEEP SIGNAL
What is a Deep Signal report?

Deep Signal is an AI-generated psychological profile of a specific prospect. It includes: why they might sell (motivation), their likely timeline, the best channel to reach them, personalized scripts for phone / letter / door, and specific language to avoid. For top Act Today prospects, we generate these overnight so they're instant. For other prospects, you can click "Generate Deep Signal" and it runs on demand in 30-60 seconds.

Can I edit a Deep Signal script?

Not yet from within the app — you can copy the scripts and adapt them however you want. Script editing and save-as-template is on the post-beta roadmap.

How accurate is Deep Signal?

Deep Signal uses public records, the scoring engine's signal analysis, and general real estate psychology knowledge — it doesn't have private information about the prospect. Treat the motivation analysis as an educated hypothesis to guide your approach, not a factual biography. Scripts are meant as starting points you can personalize further based on anything you know about the prospect from other sources.

SIX LETTERS
What is the Six Letters program?

A six-month cultivation sequence of six distinct letters designed to move a prospect from cold to listing conversation. Each letter has a specific purpose — introduction, market context, social proof, direct offer, urgency, and final ask. They're triggered by calendar and by events in the prospect's situation.

How do the Six Letters get printed and mailed?

Mail sending is handled through a print-and-mail service integrated with SellerSignal. You enroll a prospect, burn a mail credit, and the letters are printed on premium stock and mailed via USPS on schedule. You don't need to handle printing, envelopes, or stamps.

What I see in the preview — is that exactly what gets mailed?

The preview shows a templated version using the prospect's real property data. When you enroll and burn a credit, SellerSignal generates a fully bespoke AI version of all six letters using Deep Signal psychology. The bespoke versions will be different — more personalized, more specific, more compelling — but always signed with your name and brokerage.

How much does a mail credit cost?

Mail credits are priced separately from your subscription and purchased in packs. Pricing is set per-send to cover AI generation, printing, materials, and postage. See your dashboard or contact Jeremy for current pricing.

TERRITORY & BILLING
What does "exclusive territory" actually mean?

While your subscription is active and in good standing, SellerSignal will not sell access to the same ZIP code to any other real estate agent. You're the only agent in that ZIP using our intelligence. This exclusivity is tied to you personally (not your brokerage), and it applies only to other real estate agents — SellerSignal may still work with mortgage lenders, investors, or other non-agent users.

What happens if I cancel?

You retain access until the end of your current billing period. After that, your territory enters a 30-day hold where it can't be claimed by anyone else — so if you change your mind, you can reactivate without losing your spot. After the 30-day hold, the territory becomes available to new subscribers.

What if I move to a different ZIP code?

Contact us and we'll work with you to transfer your subscription. Territory transfers are handled case-by-case during beta. Post-beta there will be a formal process.

DATA & PRIVACY
Where does the property data come from?

County assessor public records. Everything you see is legally available information — owner names, addresses, assessed values, sales history, property characteristics. We do not use private or leaked data. Our contribution is in how we analyze, score, and surface this data — not in the raw data itself.

Can I export my CRM / pipeline data?

Yes. Your CRM pipeline is stored in your browser and also synced to your account. You can export the full list as CSV from the Pipeline page at any time. Upon cancellation, you have 30 days to export before data is archived.

Do you sell my outreach data to anyone?

No. Your personal outreach activity (who you contacted, notes, pipeline status) is yours. We do use anonymized, aggregated outcome data to improve our scoring models, but nothing that identifies you or your specific prospects is ever shared externally.

COMPLIANCE
Is my outreach compliant with TCPA, CAN-SPAM, and DNC rules?

Compliance is your responsibility as the licensed agent conducting the outreach. SellerSignal provides intelligence and tools but does not guarantee compliance with any federal, state, or local regulation governing real estate marketing. If you have questions about whether a specific outreach activity is compliant in your state, consult your brokerage compliance officer or a real estate attorney.

What about Fair Housing Act considerations?

All Deep Signal analysis and Six Letters content is designed to focus on property characteristics, ownership patterns, and seller motivation — never on protected class characteristics of the people involved. If you ever see language in a generated letter or Deep Signal report that seems off, please report it immediately so we can investigate. We take FHA compliance seriously.
