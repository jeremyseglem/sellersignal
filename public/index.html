DEEP SIGNAL — HARRINGTON FAMILY TRUST · 1847 BROADMOOR DR E
Seller Psychology
This is a succession-planning scenario, not financial distress. The Harrington trustees are managing a dispersed portfolio from San Francisco — 800 miles away. Decision-making is likely committee-based with multiple family members. The key emotional barrier is attachment to a family property — frame any conversation around legacy preservation, not transaction.
Recommended Approach
Lead with discretion. The Harrington family values privacy above all. Position yourself as a Broadmoor specialist who can provide a confidential market perspective. First touch should be a handwritten-style letter to the San Francisco mailing address — not a phone call. Expect a 4-6 month decision cycle with multiple family members involved.
⚠ WHAT NOT TO SAY
Don't mention the out-of-state management, the vacancy, or the trust structure. The Harringtons will feel surveilled and shut down. Position yourself as a neighborhood specialist, not someone who researched their ownership.
